## Financial Calculation Rules

### 1. Gross Revenue

Gross Revenue is simply the sum of all freight values within the selected period and filters.

**Formula:**

```text
Gross Revenue = Sum of all Freight Values
```

No additional calculations or adjustments should be applied.

---

### 2. Total Expenses

Total Expenses should only represent money that has actually left the company's bank account.

The Diesel cost must come **only** from the **Diesel** page, because that is where the actual fuel purchase is recorded.

The Diesel value stored in the **Expenses** page is only an informational field used to show how much fuel was consumed by a trip. It must **never** be included in the financial calculation of expenses.

Therefore:

```text
Total Expenses =
Total Diesel Purchases (Diesel page)
+
All Other Expenses (Expenses page)
```

The "Other Expenses" must include every expense category **except** the diesel field.

Examples:

* Driver
* Assistant
* Toll
* Maintenance
* Oil Change Installment
* DAS
* INSS
* Insurance
* Office Expenses
* Tracker
* Bsoft
* Any future expense category

**Exclude:**

* dieselRs (or any equivalent diesel field inside the Expenses table)

This field is informational only and must never be counted as money spent.

---

### 3. Net Profit

```text
Net Profit =
Gross Revenue
-
Total Expenses
```

Nothing more.

No hidden adjustments.

No duplicated calculations.

No additional deductions.

---

### 4. Profit Margin

```text
Profit Margin (%) =
(Net Profit / Gross Revenue) × 100
```

## Diesel Business Rule

This is extremely important.

The Diesel page records the **actual fuel purchase**, which is the real financial expense.

The Diesel field inside the Expenses page exists only to indicate how much diesel was consumed during a specific trip.

That value is useful for operational analysis, but it is **not** another payment made by the company.

If both values are added together, diesel is counted twice.

That is exactly what is causing the inconsistency between the application and the Excel spreadsheet.

The diesel value from the Expenses page must never be included in the financial totals.
